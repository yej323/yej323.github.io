I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mathematics at Texas A&M University, working under the supervision of Matthew Papanikolas. I obtained my bachelor's degree from the Department of Mathematics at Southern University of Science and Technology (南方科技大学).

Research interests

Number theory · Arithmetic geometry

My research lies in number theory and arithmetic geometry, with a particular emphasis on the arithmetic of function fields.

In my recent work, I use Anderson modules, shtukas, and Anderson motives to study twisted Goss L-functions, their special values, and related questions in transcendence theory.

My current research interests include:

Motives

Anderson motives over function fields and 1-motives; Nori motives over number fields.

Periods & transcendence

Special L-values, MZVs, transcendence, algebraic independence, and their motivic interpretations.

Modular forms

Classical modular forms, Drinfeld modular forms, and Drinfeld modular varieties.

Iwasawa theory

Particularly their analogues over global function fields.
