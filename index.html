I'm currently a Ph.D. candidate in Department of Mathematics at Texas A&M University, working under the supervision of Dr. Matthew Papanikolas.

I obtained my bachelor's degree in Department of Mathematics at Southern University of Science and Technology (南方科技大学).

My research lies in number theory and arithmetic geometry, with a particular emphasis on the arithmetic of function fields. In my recent work, I use Anderson modules, shtukas, and Anderson motives to study twisted Goss L-functions, their special values, and related questions in transcendence theory.

My current research interests include:

  • Motives: Anderson motives over function fields and 1-motives, Nori motives over number fields;
  • Periods and transcendence: periods, including special L-values, MZVs, transcendence and algebraic independence, and their motivic interpretations;
  • Modular forms: classical modular forms, Drinfeld modular forms, and Drinfeld modular varieties;
  • Iwasawa theory: particularly its analogues over global function fields.
